    Your first class

    1. Step 1 · 10 minutes before

      Arrive and settle in

      Grab a pair of grip socks at the desk, leave your phone in a cubby and take a minute on the bench. Your instructor will come and say hello.

    2. Step 2 · 5 minutes

      Set up your reformer

      We adjust the springs, straps and foot bar to your height and tell you exactly what each one does. Nobody expects you to know.

    3. Step 3 · 40 minutes

      Move, slowly

      Small, controlled movements with your breath. You will shake. That's the point, and it's quiet enough to hear yourself think.

    4. Step 4 · the last 5 minutes

      Lie down and rest

      Every class ends lying flat with the lights low. Then tea in the lounge, if you want to stay a little longer.

    Questions

    I have never done pilates. Is that okay?

    Yes. Most of our members started here. Book any class marked all levels and tell the instructor it's your first time; they'll set up your reformer and adjust as you go.

    What should I bring?

    Comfortable clothes and grip socks. Forgot the socks? We sell them at the desk. Water, towels and mats are on us.

    Is there parking?

    Validated parking in the building garage for 90 minutes, and the Brickell Metromover stop is across the street.
