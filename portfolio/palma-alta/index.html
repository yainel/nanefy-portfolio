Palma AltaCoral Gables

Fourteen rooms in a 1926 house, five minutes from the Venetian Pool.

A house first, a hotel second.

Built in coral rock for a family who never left, restored a century later for guests who don't want to.

The original tile, the iron gates and the courtyard banyan all stayed. We added quiet rooms, good beds and a kitchen that cooks for the people staying here, not for a crowd.

There's no lobby. Someone meets you at the gate, walks you through the loggia, and hands you a key.

A day at Palma Alta

  1. 7:30 am

    Coffee in the courtyard

    Cuban coffee and guava pastries on the long table while the banyan is still full of birds. Nobody is in a hurry, including the kitchen.

  2. Noon

    The pool, in the shade

    The courtyard pool sits under the banyan, so the water stays cool even in August. Towels, lemonade and a stack of paperbacks are already there.

  3. 6:45 pm

    Sunset on the loggia

    The arches turn pink, then gold. A glass of something cold appears, and someone from the front desk points out the best table for later.

  4. 9 pm

    Dinner under the lights

    Ten seats, one menu and a string of lights through the branches. The gate closes at midnight; your key opens it after that.
